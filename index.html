Relational Theory ongoing · mellowwei.github.io/RelaMismatch · NODE 16
Relational Dislocation Theory 关系位错论 · RDT 2.0 · interface diagnosis · NODE 16

A metacognitive and design theory for diagnosing failures that are prematurely attributed to isolated entities. Its core claim: when A fails, it does not automatically mean A is wrong. Error often belongs to the relation among A, B, interface, context, time window, and verification protocol. RDT does not deny component failure; it prevents premature component blame.

关系位错论研究的是：两个或多个单体各自可能没有坏，但它们一旦被放进同一个关系结构， 就因为接口、语境、时间窗口或验证协议不适配而共同失效。它不是取消责任，也不是把一切都说成“关系问题”； 它是在责备单体之前，先检查关系、接口、节律与验证结构。
